# Metric specification, version 0.1

All money values are stored in USD, displayed in billions on the memo, and tied to an SEC filing accession. Annual income statement and cash-flow values must span roughly 330–400 days. Direct quarterly values span roughly 70–110 days. A year-to-date Q2 or Q3 fact becomes a standalone quarter by subtracting the preceding cumulative fact with the same start date. Q4 is full-year less nine months. The two inputs remain attached to the derived point. Comparable-quarter growth uses the corresponding quarter about one year earlier.

| Output | Source fields / formula | Missing-data rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | Prefer `us-gaap:Revenues` as the reported top line; otherwise `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` or `SalesRevenueNet` | Unavailable when no comparable annual fact is mapped. The exact concept is retained in each fact record. |
| Revenue growth | Latest annual revenue / previous annual revenue − 1 | Unavailable when either amount is absent, prior revenue is zero, or FY ends are not about one year apart. |
| Gross profit | Prefer a reported `GrossProfit` fact reconciled to a compatible same-period sales and cost pair. Otherwise derive sales less `CostOfRevenue`, `CostOfGoodsAndServicesSold`, or `CostOfGoodsSold` from the same accession. | Unavailable when the same-period and same-filing basis cannot be established, a reported gross-profit line does not reconcile, or the appropriate sales base is ambiguous. |
| Gross margin | Gross profit / compatible sales base, with both the gross-profit and base fact IDs retained | Unavailable when gross profit or its compatible denominator is missing. The base can differ from headline revenue: Walmart uses net sales for gross margin, while the headline revenue metric uses total revenues. |
| EBITDA | Prefer an exact EBITDA fact with a matching unadjusted label in an annual 10-K; otherwise `OperatingIncomeLoss` + separately mapped depreciation and amortization | The first is labeled issuer-reported EBITDA, never covenant EBITDA. The fallback is explicitly **estimated EBITDA**. Both are unavailable when their same-period inputs are missing. A combined “depreciation, amortization, and other” line is insufficient for the estimate. |
| EBITDA margin | Available EBITDA / revenue | Unavailable unless both aligned values exist and revenue is nonzero; its source follows the reported-or-estimated EBITDA basis. |
| Cash | `CashAndCashEquivalentsAtCarryingValue` at the latest filing date | Unavailable if no tagged cash fact exists. Cash restrictions are researched separately. |
| Debt | Short-term borrowings, if tagged, + current and noncurrent long-term debt at one balance-sheet date | If either long-term component is missing, unavailable. If no short-term fact is mapped, label the result **long-term debt subtotal**, not comprehensive debt. Lease obligations are excluded. |
| Gross leverage estimate | Included debt / same-date issuer-reported FY EBITDA when verified; otherwise four consecutive standalone quarters of estimated EBITDA ending on the debt date, or same-date FY estimated EBITDA | Unavailable if the debt and EBITDA dates differ, a required period is missing, or EBITDA is not positive. This is not a covenant ratio. |
| Inventory days on hand (optional) | Average of current/prior FY `InventoryNet` balances / same-period reported cost of sales × actual FY days | Unavailable without two comparable FY balance dates or a compatible cost fact. |
| Receivables days on hand (optional) | Average of current/prior FY net trade receivables / compatible sales base × actual FY days | Unavailable without two comparable FY balance dates or the cited sales base. Broad receivables aggregates are not treated as trade receivables. |
| Fixed charge coverage | Definition pending | Always unavailable until Ashley approves numerator, denominator, and required debt-service inputs. |

The SEC filing URL, accession, filed date, concept, unit, start/end dates, and value are retained for every selected input. Same-date income statement components must also have identical start dates; FY and quarter calculations do not mix overlapping periods. A metric contains its formula and input fact IDs. Gross-margin trend points also retain the compatible denominator and its fact IDs. Insights contain public source IDs. Every generated memo stays in draft until an analyst verifies all insights. Industry text has a separate licensed source and is never included in the AI input.

Known limitations: standardized XBRL tags vary across issuers; tag mapping must be checked against the actual filing in each pilot. Issuer-reported EBITDA may have a company-specific non-GAAP definition and must be reviewed before comparison; the calculator only accepts an unadjusted exact EBITDA label from a 10-K. Debt omits leases, and an untagged short-term facility may be missed. The leverage ratio is a screening calculation, not a credit agreement definition.
